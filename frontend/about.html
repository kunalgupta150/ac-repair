About AC CARE

Cool Comfort, Our Priority. We help customers keep their air conditioners running smoothly through convenient repair, cleaning and maintenance services.

Who We Are

AC CARE is an air-conditioning service platform designed to make booking and managing AC services easier. Customers can request services, track bookings and stay updated, while administrators coordinate service requests and technicians.

🎯 Our Mission

To make AC repair and maintenance convenient, organised and accessible for customers.

🌟 Our Vision

To provide a simple and dependable way for customers to manage their air-conditioning service needs.

🤝 Why Choose Us?

Easy booking, clear service updates and an organised process for managing service requests.
